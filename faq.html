Maintaining accurate observation data

How do I prevent catalog drift over time?
Establish regular audit cadences and record explicit observation timestamps on every saved row so you can quickly identify which product parameters require fresh verification before submitting orders.
What is the protocol for discontinued items?
Mark the row as archived with the date observed and link to an alternative merchant source rather than deleting the row, preserving valuable historical pricing and sizing context.
How should currency conversions be handled?
Record the native RMB price first; currency conversions should be calculated dynamically using current commercial rates from your payment provider rather than fixed estimates.

Warehouse inspection and quality control

What details must be checked upon warehouse arrival?
Check physical ruler measurements against the seller size chart, inspect fabric seams and hardware tags, and confirm that the delivered item matches the exact SKU option ordered.
How long do domestic return windows typically remain open?
Most marketplace sellers allow returns within designated merchant policy windows after delivery; inspect warehouse photos promptly to request exchanges before deadlines lapse.
Can supplementary inspection angles be requested?
Yes, you can request targeted photos of specific tags, embroidery alignment, or sole measurements through the agent portal to resolve ambiguous quality questions.

International logistics and customs preparation

How does volumetric weight impact freight costs?
Carriers charge on the greater of actual scale weight and volumetric size calculated as length times width times height divided by the route divisor; compact packing saves money.
When should shoe boxes or retail packaging be removed?
Removing bulky packaging reduces parcel volume significantly, lowering shipping costs on volumetric lines while simple bubble wrapping protects items during air transit.
Is shipping insurance recommended for international parcels?
Insurance is useful for high-value hauls to protect against transit loss, severe damage, or customs delays, offering peace of mind for a modest percentage of parcel value.
