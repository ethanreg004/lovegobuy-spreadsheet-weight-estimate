Execution Protocol

Systematic four-stage workflow

Execute each phase in sequential order to maintain absolute data integrity across your product catalog and streamline international forwarding operations.

  1. 1. Step 1: Source URL check

    Navigate to the final merchant product page, confirming URL stability and stripping session tracking parameters so links remain persistent across team members.

  2. 2. Step 2: Option and specification capture

    Transcribe size identifiers, color codes, and batch numbers verbatim from the live selection interface to ensure purchasing agents buy the exact targeted variant.

  3. 3. Step 3: Evidence documentation and stamping

    Log the observed price, currency, and inspection photo requirements with an explicit observation timestamp to maintain historical audit boundaries over time.

  4. 4. Step 4: Audit review and catalog integration

    Review all four checkpoints against the master spreadsheet schema and assign a future recheck date before clearing the row for active order submission.
